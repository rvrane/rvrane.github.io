About

I am a genomics professional based in Ahmedabad, India, with experience across clinical diagnostics, reproductive genetics, and bioinformatics-driven product development. My work spans copy number analysis from shallow whole-genome sequencing, variant calling from exome and genome data, assay design for reproductive and oncology use cases, and bioinformatics infrastructure that labs can actually use.

Clinical genomics

Experience in CAP/CLIA-aligned environments, germline and somatic analysis, interpretation, and reporting-oriented workflows.
